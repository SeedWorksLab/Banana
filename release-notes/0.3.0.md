### Offline Whisper dictation

- Choose Android's offline speech recognizer or downloadable multilingual Whisper Base and Small models in Settings → Dictation.
- Set the spoken language independently of the app language, or use automatic language detection with Whisper.
- Whisper records first and transcribes locally after Stop. Audio is not uploaded for speech recognition; subsequent AI actions still use your configured provider.
- Model downloads require confirmation, show progress, support cancellation and verify file checksums. Models are kept in private storage across normal app updates, excluded from backups and household sync, and never downloaded automatically.

### More varied meal recommendations

- Weekly planning prioritises different suitable recipes and balances repeats when choices are limited, rather than repeatedly favouring the same pantry-friendly or discounted dish.
- Recent planned meals provide a four-week cooldown, even if they have not been marked cooked or rated. Weekly tie-breaking avoids permanently favouring the same recipes.
- Pinned meals are reserved before automatic choices, and scarce quick recipes are saved for meals with tighter cooking-time limits.
- Near-identical ingredient combinations receive less preference. “Try something new” also mixes meal types and ingredients while excluding incompatible diets, allergens, dislikes, inactive recipes and placeholder wishes.
- Existing pins, cooked meals and explicit leftover reservations remain protected. Tap Plan my week to regenerate automatic meals.

### Clearer leftover planning

- Choose exactly which earlier cooking meal supplies a leftover meal, including different meal types and non-consecutive days within the same week and home.
- Adjust the portions reserved for each meal. Cooking totals update when reservations are resized, moved or removed, while the shopping list counts ingredients only at the cooking meal.
- The plan clearly separates portions to cook, portions to eat and portions saved for later, and names the source of each leftover meal.
- Editing cooking portions preserves valid leftover links. Already cooked or eaten batches are protected from resizing and reassignment.

### A tidier plan and settings

- Expand or collapse individual day cards. Past dates start collapsed; today and future dates start expanded. Your choices are remembered while browsing weeks in the current screen session.
- Settings now uses clearer groups, with theme, interface language and currency alongside Preferences & defaults.
- Check for updates is a rounded inline toggle.
- Removed the separate in-app Plan updates notification switch. Android notification permission remains under your control, and a notification failure cannot undo a completed plan update.

### Interface fixes

- Fixed web dropdown errors caused by nested buttons.
- Dictation and other long-text fields now keep their labels and outlines stationary while only the text scrolls. Labels no longer jump from the middle of the field, scrollbars stay within the box, and forced scroll-to-end jumps have been removed.
