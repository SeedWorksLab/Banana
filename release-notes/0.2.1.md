### Cook once, eat over several days

- Choose how many days to cook for when editing a meal. Banana reserves the following meals as leftovers and includes the extra portions in your shopping list.
- Reserved leftovers stay linked to their cooking day, and uneaten reservations are cleared if that meal changes.

### SeedWorks and shared life

- SeedWorks connection settings are included in the app, with automatic foreground syncing, account-wide usernames and usage reporting.
- Invite another SeedWorks account by email to share recipes, homes, pantries and meal plans. Invitations require acceptance.
- Personal data stays separate from household data and returns when you leave the household or sign out.

### Updates and everyday fixes

- Enable startup update checks to see new official releases. Dismiss a prompt for now, ignore that version, or download its APK with Android progress notifications and installation confirmation.
- AI requests now have a firm timeout instead of waiting indefinitely for a stalled provider.
- Dictation checks installed offline speech languages and regional variants rather than forcing a specific language pack.
- Welcome and loading screens use the actual Banana logo.
- Long prompts, notes and recipe steps stay inside fixed-height scrolling fields, keeping submission controls accessible.
